# Mall Agro Positioning Baseline

## Company Role

Mall Agro is positioned on the website as a focused B2B equipment catalog and inquiry starting point for agriculture, grain processing and handling, and food industry equipment.

The website should help visitors choose the relevant equipment area, understand the working context for that area, and prepare the information needed for a focused equipment inquiry.

Mall Agro content should support qualified inquiries rather than WooCommerce-style browsing, product-stock browsing, or instant checkout behavior.

## What Mall Agro Is

Mall Agro is:

- a modern B2B equipment catalog for agriculture, grain processing and handling, and food industry equipment;
- inquiry-driven, with the quote path used to collect project and equipment context;
- project-oriented, helping visitors describe workflows, materials, site limits, and expected equipment needs;
- category-led, with navigation organized around three approved equipment categories;
- conservative in claims, using only source-approved business facts and avoiding unsupported performance, partnership, or market claims.

## What Mall Agro Is Not

Mall Agro is not:

- an ecommerce checkout experience;
- a marketplace;
- a product stock portal;
- an instant quotation system;
- a seed-heavy catalog based on the old WooCommerce taxonomy;
- a source for unsupported product availability, exact model, capacity, certification, distributor, or market-leadership claims.

## Core Markets

The approved market and category scope is:

- Agriculture
- Grain Processing / Grain Handling
- Food Industry Equipment

The approved domain model is:

- `mallagro.com` for English content;
- `mallagro.ro` for Romanian content.

Geographic service coverage, international manufacturer relationships, partner networks, official representation, and delivery coverage must not be claimed unless separately approved from validated sources.

## Customer Types

Approved customer groups include:

- agricultural businesses;
- grain processors and handlers;
- food industry operators;
- procurement and operations managers;
- technical buyers looking for equipment, automation, or machinery solutions;
- project and procurement teams preparing equipment requirements;
- farms, mills, storage operators, processors, and food production companies when the context is tied to the approved categories.

## Equipment Scope

### Agriculture

Agriculture content may cover field activity, preparation tasks, material handling, agricultural workflows, site realities, and equipment planning around farming-related work.

### Grain Processing / Grain Handling

Grain Processing / Grain Handling content may cover grain reception, cleaning and preparation, conveying and elevation, storage-adjacent handling, grain flow, and process planning.

### Food Industry Equipment

Food Industry Equipment content may cover food production workflows, preparation and handling steps, processing environments, production line planning, operational continuity needs, and equipment planning for food production contexts.

## Inquiry Philosophy

Mall Agro inquiries should be structured around practical context, not around instant product purchase behavior.

Visitors should be encouraged to prepare:

- the relevant equipment category;
- operation type or production activity;
- material, grain type, or food product type involved;
- current workflow or process steps;
- site limits, available space, access, or integration needs if known;
- expected equipment type or equipment need;
- questions that should guide the first planning discussion.

The inquiry path should help the first response focus on relevant details. It must not promise immediate pricing, guaranteed availability, or guaranteed project outcomes.

## Trust Themes

Approved trust-building themes are:

- clear B2B category organization;
- disciplined separation from the old WooCommerce taxonomy;
- practical inquiry preparation;
- source-verified content and claim discipline;
- English/Romanian domain and localization integrity;
- explicit route and language-pair handling rather than guessed localization;
- transparent temporary `noindex` treatment for pages that are not final SEO-ready pages;
- structured category pages that explain scope, applications, equipment needs, and inquiry preparation.

## Claims To Avoid

Do not claim or imply:

- official representative status;
- exclusive distributor status;
- named manufacturer partnerships unless explicitly approved;
- guaranteed delivery across Romania, Europe, or the EU;
- market leadership;
- certifications or compliance claims;
- exact years of experience;
- product availability or stock status;
- exact machine models, specifications, capacities, or performance metrics unless source-verified;
- guaranteed efficiency, production, savings, or project outcomes;
- instant quotation or instant equipment selection;
- ecommerce checkout behavior;
- old WooCommerce seed taxonomy as the new product structure;
- Romanian content, slugs, or metadata on the English domain;
- English content, slugs, or metadata on the Romanian domain.

## Future Homepage Messaging Direction

### Hero Positioning

The homepage hero should present Mall Agro as a B2B equipment category gateway for agriculture, grain processing and handling, and food industry equipment.

Hero copy should stay clear, direct, and conservative. It should not use unsupported claims such as "expert solutions", years of experience, market leadership, official representation, or guaranteed outcomes.

### Capability Positioning

Homepage capability messaging should explain that Mall Agro helps visitors organize equipment needs around practical workflows, materials, site context, and project requirements.

The homepage may describe support for category selection and inquiry preparation. It should not claim final product selection, guaranteed engineering outcomes, certified delivery, or official partner status without approved source validation.

### Inquiry Positioning

Homepage inquiry messaging should explain what visitors should share when requesting a quote: category, operation, material or product type, current workflow, site constraints, and expected equipment need.

The quote path should be described as a focused inquiry starting point, not a final sales system, product stock checker, or instant quotation tool.
