# Homepage Messaging Framework

## Status

Working draft v1.

This document defines the approved messaging direction for the Mall Agro homepage before implementation.

The homepage must position Mall Agro as a B2B equipment and project partner, not only as a product catalog.

## Core Homepage Role

The homepage should introduce Mall Agro as a trusted starting point for equipment inquiries across:

* Agriculture
* Grain Processing / Grain Handling
* Food Industry Equipment

The catalog structure is a navigation tool.
The business message is broader: Mall Agro helps visitors organize equipment needs, project requirements, and inquiry details before moving toward a quote discussion.

## Primary Positioning

Mall Agro should be presented as:

**A B2B equipment and project partner for agriculture, grain handling, and food production operations.**

The homepage should not communicate Mall Agro as:

* a simple webshop;
* a product stock portal;
* an instant quotation system;
* a generic product directory;
* a tractor or single-machine dealer.

## What The Visitor Should Understand In 5 Seconds

A visitor should quickly understand:

1. Mall Agro works in agriculture, grain handling, and food industry equipment.
2. The company supports project-oriented equipment inquiries.
3. The website helps visitors choose the right category and prepare a focused inquiry.
4. The next step is not checkout, but a structured quote discussion.

## Homepage Message Hierarchy

### Level 1 — Company Role

Mall Agro supports equipment planning and inquiry preparation for agricultural and food-related operations.

### Level 2 — Category Scope

The website is organized around three main equipment areas:

* Agriculture
* Grain Processing / Grain Handling
* Food Industry Equipment

### Level 3 — Capability

Mall Agro helps structure equipment needs around:

* operation type;
* material or product flow;
* current workflow;
* site limitations;
* expected equipment requirements;
* project or production context.

### Level 4 — Inquiry

The quote path should help the visitor share enough context for a focused first discussion.

## Hero Messaging Direction

### Preferred Hero Headline Direction

**Equipment Support for Agriculture, Grain Handling and Food Production Projects**

Alternative:

**B2B Equipment Support for Agricultural and Food Processing Operations**

### Hero Supporting Copy Direction

The supporting copy should explain that visitors can explore the main equipment areas, define their operational needs, and start a focused inquiry.

Example direction:

**Explore equipment areas, review the working context, and prepare a focused inquiry for agriculture, grain handling, or food production requirements.**

### Hero CTA Direction

Primary CTA:

**Explore Equipment Areas**

Secondary CTA:

**Request a Quote**

The hero should not use phrases such as:

* market leader;
* best solution;
* guaranteed performance;
* official representative;
* exclusive distributor;
* instant quotation;
* EU-wide guaranteed delivery.

## Trust Messaging Direction

The homepage should build trust through clarity and professional discipline, not through exaggerated claims.

Approved trust themes:

* structured B2B category organization;
* project-oriented inquiry preparation;
* practical equipment requirement gathering;
* clear separation from ecommerce checkout behavior;
* conservative, source-safe claims;
* English/Romanian domain and language discipline.

Possible trust block title:

**Built Around Practical Equipment Inquiries**

Possible supporting idea:

Mall Agro helps visitors organize the first steps of an equipment inquiry by category, workflow, material, site context, and expected equipment need.

## Capability Messaging Direction

The homepage should explain what Mall Agro helps the visitor do.

Recommended capability pillars:

### Identify the relevant equipment area

Visitors should be guided toward Agriculture, Grain Processing / Grain Handling, or Food Industry Equipment.

### Clarify the working context

The page should encourage visitors to think about operation type, material flow, production activity, site conditions, and workflow needs.

### Prepare a focused inquiry

The quote process should be framed as a way to share relevant details before a project or equipment planning discussion.

### Move from category interest to practical requirements

The homepage should connect browsing with real inquiry preparation.

## Category Gateway Positioning

The category section should remain central, but it should not feel like the whole homepage.

Its role is to help visitors choose the most relevant starting point.

### Agriculture

Position as the starting point for field activity, preparation tasks, material handling, site realities, and agricultural equipment requirements.

### Grain Processing / Grain Handling

Position as the starting point for grain reception, cleaning, conveying, elevation, storage-adjacent handling, and grain flow discussions.

### Food Industry Equipment

Position as the starting point for production workflows, processing environments, preparation and handling steps, line planning, and operational continuity.

## Project Support Process

The homepage should explain a simple inquiry path:

1. Choose the relevant equipment area.
2. Describe the operation, material, or production activity.
3. Share workflow, site, or process requirements.
4. Start a focused quote discussion.

This process should feel practical, not bureaucratic.

## CTA Messaging Direction

The final CTA should not only say “Request a quote.”

It should explain what the visitor should share and why.

Recommended CTA direction:

**Planning an equipment inquiry?**

Supporting copy:

**Share the category, operation, material or product type, current workflow, site limits, and expected equipment need so the first discussion can start with relevant details.**

CTA label:

**Request a Quote**

## Tone Of Voice Rules

The homepage voice should be:

* professional;
* calm;
* practical;
* B2B-oriented;
* clear;
* confident without overclaiming;
* human, not corporate-buzzword heavy.

Avoid:

* hype;
* exaggerated confidence;
* vague “solutions for everything” language;
* generic AI phrasing;
* unsupported leadership claims;
* fake certainty.

## Forbidden Messaging

Do not claim or imply:

* official representative status;
* exclusive distributor status;
* named manufacturer partnerships unless approved;
* guaranteed delivery across Romania, Europe, or the EU;
* market leadership;
* certifications;
* exact years of experience;
* product availability or stock;
* exact machine models, capacities, or performance metrics unless source-verified;
* guaranteed project outcomes;
* instant quotation;
* ecommerce checkout behavior.

## Implementation Guidance

Future homepage implementation should:

* preserve the three-category structure;
* strengthen the company and trust message before or around the category gateway;
* remove wording that makes the site feel like a simple catalog;
* avoid unsupported claims;
* keep the quote path as inquiry preparation, not instant sales.

## Open Editorial Questions

Before final homepage implementation, decide whether the homepage may safely mention:

1. international equipment manufacturers;
2. partner network;
3. Romania as a priority market;
4. broader European project context;
5. trade/procurement support.

These should not be added to public copy until approved as source-safe.
