# Homepage Information Architecture

## Status

Approved working direction v1.

This document defines the intended information architecture for the Mall Agro homepage before a future homepage rebuild or major layout refinement.

The current homepage should not be treated as final. It is a working baseline that helped clarify what is missing.

## Strategic Decision

Mall Agro must be positioned primarily as:

**An equipment and industrial project partner for agriculture, grain processing, and food production.**

The product catalog is a tool inside that positioning.

Mall Agro should not be positioned primarily as:

* a webshop;
* a simple product catalog;
* a stock portal;
* a generic machine dealer;
* a directory of equipment categories.

## Core Business Meaning

Mall Agro helps investors and producers build, modernize, and develop production operations in:

* agriculture;
* grain processing and handling;
* food industry equipment.

The company can support both:

* single-machine or smaller equipment requests;
* complete equipment solutions for more complex industrial projects.

This dual capability is important: smaller equipment requests support regular business flow, while larger project-oriented work represents the strongest strategic value.

## Primary Homepage Goal

The homepage should answer one question before anything else:

**Why should a serious buyer speak with Mall Agro?**

Only after this question is answered should the page guide visitors toward equipment categories.

## Current Homepage Problem

The current homepage still behaves too much like:

**Hero → Categories → Inquiry**

This makes the page feel catalog-first.

The desired direction is:

**Company role → Project capability → Equipment scope → Inquiry path**

The homepage should communicate that Mall Agro is a serious business partner before asking the visitor to choose a category.

## Target Visitor Groups

The homepage must speak to two primary visitor types.

### Strategic Project Buyer

This includes:

* investors;
* business owners;
* plant operators;
* project managers;
* technical decision-makers;
* procurement teams.

Typical needs:

* new production line;
* new facility;
* modernization project;
* process upgrade;
* storage or handling system;
* integrated equipment solution.

This visitor is the highest strategic value.

### Equipment Buyer

This includes:

* small and medium producers;
* farms;
* processors;
* facility operators;
* buyers looking for individual machines or targeted upgrades.

Typical needs:

* single machine;
* replacement equipment;
* process improvement;
* smaller handling or production solution;
* specific equipment inquiry.

This visitor remains important because smaller requests support regular commercial activity.

## Core Homepage Story

The homepage should tell this story:

1. Mall Agro supports agricultural, grain processing, and food production projects.
2. The company helps clients move from a business or production need toward equipment requirements.
3. The company can support both single equipment needs and broader industrial projects.
4. Mall Agro brings together equipment selection, project structuring, logistics coordination, and implementation support.
5. The category catalog helps visitors start in the right area.
6. The inquiry path helps collect the practical details needed for a focused first discussion.

## Target Homepage Section Order

The recommended homepage structure is:

1. Hero
2. Company Positioning / Why Mall Agro
3. What We Support
4. Project Capabilities
5. Equipment Categories
6. Inquiry Process
7. Final CTA

This order is intentional.

The categories should not appear before the company/project-partner message is established.

## Section 1 — Hero

### Purpose

Make the visitor immediately understand that Mall Agro is more than a product catalog.

### Communication Goal

Present Mall Agro as an equipment and project support partner for agriculture, grain processing, and food production.

### Recommended Message Direction

Headline direction:

**Industrial Equipment and Project Support for Agriculture, Grain Processing and Food Production**

Alternative:

**Equipment and Project Solutions for Agricultural and Food Processing Operations**

Supporting copy direction:

Mall Agro helps investors and producers structure equipment needs, plan production workflows, and prepare focused project or machine inquiries.

### CTA Direction

Primary CTA:

**Explore Capabilities**

Secondary CTA:

**Request a Quote**

Avoid making the first CTA only category-focused.

## Section 2 — Company Positioning / Why Mall Agro

### Purpose

Explain why a serious buyer should speak with Mall Agro.

### Communication Goal

Show that Mall Agro supports equipment decisions, project structuring, logistics, and implementation — not only product browsing.

### Possible Content Pillars

* Project-oriented approach
* Equipment selection support
* Workflow and requirement clarification
* Logistics coordination
* Implementation support

### Claim Safety

Do not claim market leadership, guaranteed results, official representation, exclusive distributor status, or named manufacturer partnerships unless separately approved.

## Section 3 — What We Support

### Purpose

Show the two levels of business support.

### Structure

Use two main paths.

#### Single Equipment Needs

For smaller or focused requirements:

* individual machines;
* replacement equipment;
* small production upgrades;
* targeted handling or processing needs.

#### Industrial Projects

For larger or more complex requirements:

* new production lines;
* modernization projects;
* silo and storage systems;
* grain handling and processing systems;
* process lines from raw material intake to packaging where source-safe;
* integrated industrial equipment solutions.

### Communication Goal

Make it clear that Mall Agro is relevant both to smaller buyers and to serious industrial project buyers.

## Section 4 — Project Capabilities

### Purpose

Build trust without exposing sensitive project names, manufacturers, or client identities.

### Communication Goal

Show capability through types of work, not through confidential project evidence.

### Approved Capability Themes

* project planning support;
* equipment selection;
* engineering coordination;
* transport and logistics organization;
* implementation and installation support;
* multidisciplinary team involvement;
* support across agriculture, grain processing, and food industry contexts.

### Sensitive Information Rule

Because Mall Agro may act as subcontractor or partner in larger projects, public website content must avoid exposing:

* client names;
* project locations when sensitive;
* visible manufacturer names;
* supplier relationships that are not approved for publication;
* confidential project photos;
* exact project details that may allow bypassing Mall Agro.

## Section 5 — Equipment Categories

### Purpose

Help visitors choose the right starting area after the business role has been established.

### Categories

* Agriculture
* Grain Processing / Grain Handling
* Food Industry Equipment

### Communication Goal

Categories are not the whole homepage. They are entry points into equipment and project discussions.

### Strategic Priority

Grain Processing / Grain Handling is the most strategically important category and should receive strong attention in future content, visual direction, and product architecture.

## Section 6 — Inquiry Process

### Purpose

Explain how the visitor moves from interest to productive first discussion.

### Recommended Steps

1. Define the business or production need.
2. Identify the relevant equipment area.
3. Share material, workflow, site, and process details.
4. Start a focused quote or project discussion.

### Communication Goal

The inquiry process should feel practical and professional, not like checkout or instant quotation.

## Section 7 — Final CTA

### Purpose

Encourage contact while preserving the project-oriented positioning.

### Recommended CTA Direction

Headline:

**Planning a Machine Request or Industrial Project?**

Supporting copy:

Share the operation, material or product type, current workflow, site limits, and expected equipment need so Mall Agro can review the inquiry with the right context.

CTA label:

**Request a Quote**

## Trust Strategy Without Exposing Sensitive Projects

Mall Agro should build trust through:

* capability descriptions;
* types of projects supported;
* process understanding;
* engineering and logistics role;
* multidisciplinary team framing;
* category expertise;
* careful inquiry preparation.

Avoid relying on:

* public project galleries;
* client logos;
* named machine manufacturers;
* detailed case studies;
* exact project references;
* numbers that are not source-approved.

## What The Homepage Must Stop Doing

The homepage must stop behaving primarily like a category index.

Avoid this story:

**Choose a category → send inquiry**

Prefer this story:

**Understand Mall Agro’s role → see what the company supports → choose a category → prepare a focused inquiry**

## Content Tone

The homepage should sound:

* serious;
* technical;
* clear;
* project-oriented;
* practical;
* trustworthy;
* European B2B;
* confident without overclaiming.

Avoid:

* generic “solutions for everything” language;
* marketing hype;
* fake authority;
* unsupported global claims;
* casual consumer language;
* ecommerce wording;
* instant quote language.

## Forbidden Public Claims

Do not claim or imply:

* official representative status unless approved;
* exclusive distributor status;
* guaranteed delivery across Romania, Europe, or the EU;
* named manufacturer partnerships unless approved;
* market leadership;
* certifications;
* exact years of experience;
* guaranteed project outcomes;
* stock availability;
* exact machine models or capacities unless source-approved;
* confidential project references;
* client names or project images without approval.

## Implementation Notes

Future implementation should not be a minor copy polish of the current homepage.

It should be treated as a homepage architecture refinement.

The main implementation change should be section order and narrative structure:

Current baseline:

**Hero → Categories → Company/Authority → Inquiry**

Target direction:

**Hero → Company Positioning → What We Support → Project Capabilities → Categories → Inquiry → CTA**
