# Homepage v2 Content Matrix

## Status

Working draft v1.

This document defines the approved content direction for the Mall Agro homepage v2.

The purpose is to stop broad generative rewriting and give implementation agents exact section intent, copy direction, visual intent, and claim-safety boundaries.

## Core Direction

Mall Agro must be presented as:

**An industrial equipment and project support partner for agriculture, grain processing, and food production.**

The product catalog is a navigation and inquiry tool. It is not the full business story.

Homepage v2 must communicate:

* individual equipment needs;
* production lines;
* modernization work;
* industrial equipment solutions;
* logistics coordination;
* implementation context;
* project-oriented support.

## Approved Stronger Language

The following terms are approved for homepage v2 when used carefully:

* production lines;
* industrial equipment solutions;
* modernization projects;
* equipment selection;
* production workflow;
* material flow;
* logistics coordination;
* implementation support;
* integrated equipment solutions;
* project-oriented support.

## Forbidden Language

Do not claim or imply:

* market leader;
* official representative status;
* exclusive distributor status;
* named manufacturer partnerships unless separately approved;
* guaranteed EU delivery;
* guaranteed project delivery;
* turnkey delivery unless explicitly approved later;
* EPC status unless explicitly approved later;
* stock availability;
* instant quotation;
* exact capacities;
* exact machine models;
* client names;
* confidential project references.

## Homepage v2 Target Order

Homepage v2 should follow this order:

1. Hero
2. Company Positioning / Why Mall Agro
3. What We Support
4. Project Capabilities
5. Equipment Categories
6. Authority / Trust Discipline
7. Inquiry Process
8. Final CTA

The category gateway must not be the main story of the homepage. It is a tool after the company/project value is established.

# Section 2 - Company Positioning / Why Mall Agro

## Section Goal

Explain why a serious buyer should speak with Mall Agro before choosing a machine or supplier.

## Exact Content Direction

### Eyebrow

Why Mall Agro

### Title

From equipment requests to production-ready decisions

### Body

Mall Agro helps clients move from a business or production need toward a clearer equipment path. The company supports discussions around machinery, production lines, material handling, workflow requirements and project context before a focused quote discussion begins.

For smaller buyers, this may mean identifying a suitable machine or upgrade. For larger projects, it may mean coordinating equipment requirements, logistics considerations and implementation context around a broader industrial solution.

## Suggested Support Points

* Equipment needs linked to real operating context
* Support for both individual machines and broader projects
* Practical preparation before quotation
* Category structure used as a starting point, not as the full service

## Visual Intent

This section should feel like a company positioning block, not a catalog explanation.

Possible visual treatment:

* split text with a capability panel;
* minimal icon set;
* project flow graphic;
* neutral industrial background panel.

## Claim-Safety Notes

Do not claim full engineering responsibility unless approved. Do not claim turnkey execution.

# Section 7 - Inquiry Process

## Section Goal

Explain the path from business need to useful first discussion.

## Exact Content Direction

### Eyebrow

Inquiry process

### Title

From production need to focused project discussion

### Supporting Copy

The inquiry path helps turn a broad equipment interest into the practical details needed for a first review.

## Steps

### Step 1 - Define the production or operational need

Start from the work that needs to be done: agricultural operation, grain flow, production activity, handling step or process upgrade.

### Step 2 - Identify the relevant equipment area

Choose whether the inquiry belongs under Agriculture, Grain Processing / Grain Handling, or Food Industry Equipment.

### Step 3 - Share material, workflow and site conditions

Prepare information about the material or product type, current process, available space, access, site limits and expected equipment role.

### Step 4 - Start a focused quote or project discussion

Use the quote path to share enough context for a more relevant first response.

## Visual Intent

This section should remain clear and process-oriented. It should not dominate the homepage.

## Claim-Safety Notes

Do not imply instant quotation or guaranteed equipment selection.
